## 数据源

```sql
SELECT id, direction, message_type, content_text, received_at
FROM messages
WHERE (sender_identity_id = ? AND receiver_identity_id = ?)
   OR (sender_identity_id = ? AND receiver_identity_id = ?)
  AND message_type = 'text'
ORDER BY received_at DESC
LIMIT 50
```
